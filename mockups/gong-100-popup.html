Bartenders: the live clock and shimmer mean it's the real deal, not a screenshot.

WHAT HAPPENS

The roll saves, and the server notices this person is the 100th user ever to earn the Hit the Gong achievement. Instead of the normal achievement modal, the app plays this sequence: the screen dims, a gold gong drops in, a mallet strikes it, the screen flashes, shockwaves ripple out, and confetti fires while the counter races from #1 up to #100.

THE PASS

The gold ticket is what the bartender looks at. It shows the roller's name, the gong number, and a ticking live clock under a moving holographic shimmer, so a bartender can tell at a glance that it's the real app and not a screenshot someone passed around.

REDEEMING

The green button is optional: the bartender presses and holds it for about a second and a half, the ticket gets stamped REDEEMED, and it can't be used twice. Try holding it in the mockup. If the pass is closed before redemption, it stays reachable from the user's profile until it's redeemed.
